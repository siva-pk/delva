# D-19 — Community

**Status: approach only. No accounts created, nothing posted anywhere, by anyone or anything.**

This is the item that most needs a person and least tolerates automation. It is written down so
there is a plan to argue with, not so it can be executed by a script.

## Why this is the constraint

deskflo's own analytics settled it: Reddit produced 36 sessions and 329 events; Google produced 10
sessions and 30 events. Roughly **ten times the engagement per visit from community versus SEO**,
and that was without participating properly — just links.

Traffic is the binding constraint on Delva, and this is where the traffic is.

## The rule

**Participate as a person who has this problem, not as a founder with a launch.** The ADHD
communities have seen every productivity app that ever wanted their attention, and they are
extremely good at spotting the ones that see them as a market. Getting this wrong doesn't produce
zero — it produces a reputation that follows the product name.

Concretely:
- Answer questions for weeks before mentioning the product at all.
- Link the *research posts*, not the app, and only where they actually answer the question asked.
- When Delva is relevant, say plainly that you built it. Never pretend to be a happy user.
- Read each community's self-promotion rules and follow them exactly.

## Where

Left deliberately unresearched. Naming specific subreddits and Discords from a general impression is
how you end up posting in the wrong place — the list should come from lurking, not from a guess made
by a build script.

## What to listen for

More valuable than any signup: **do people describe the estimate as friction?** D-06's take-up rate
answers this in aggregate, but the community answers *why*. If the first three people who try it say
"I skipped the estimate part", that is the strategy-level finding arriving early and cheap.

## Explicitly not doing

- No automated posting, scheduling or cross-posting.
- No accounts created on Siva's behalf.
- No engagement-farming, no seeded reviews, no sockpuppets.
