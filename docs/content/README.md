## The one rule for all of it

**Every claim traces to a citation in `docs/research/focus-science.md`.** If a post makes a claim
that file doesn't support, the post is wrong and gets cut — not softened. The credibility of the
research brief is the whole asset; one overreaching claim spends it.

And the standing guardrails apply to writing exactly as they do to code: no efficacy claims for
focus sounds, no treatment or medical claims, and nothing that frames ADHD as a deficit to be fixed
by a timer.
